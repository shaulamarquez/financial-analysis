## Overview

A project that utilizes Microsoft Power BI to analyze the financial data and visualize with ineteractive dashboard. 

The dataset includes the following:

* General Ledger table

* Chart of Accounts table

* Territory table

* Calendar table

* Cashflow Statement table

* Statement of Changes in Equity table

## Financial Intelligence: Beyond the Balance Sheet

The Core Challenge: "Data Wealth, Insight Poverty"
Most executives have access to their numbers, but they lack the story behind them. Without this dashboard, leadership often faces:

The Consolidation Lag: Waiting days or weeks for manual reports to be compiled from the General Ledger, Chart of Accounts, and Territory tables.

Margin Erosion: Seeing a rise in sales but being unable to immediately tell if Net Profit Margin is keeping pace (e.g., noticing the dip from 22.87% in 2019 to 16.46% in 2020).

Operating Expense "Bloat": Difficulty tracking exactly where overhead is scaling faster than revenue across Marketing or Administration.

Static Forecasting: The inability to "filter" by country or year instantly to see how specific territories are impacting global P&L.

## Strategic Solutions Delivered
1. Real-Time Margin Transparency

The Pain: "We’re selling more, but why is our take-home pay shrinking?"

The Fix: Integrated Gross vs. Net Profit Margin tracking. This dashboard highlights when Cost of Sales or Operating Expenses are outperforming Revenue growth, allowing for immediate course correction.

2. Expense Governance & Marketing ROI

The Pain: "Is our marketing spend actually driving sales?"

The Fix: A dedicated Sales to Marketing Cost visual. By overlaying these metrics, we can see the direct correlation between spend and yield, preventing "blind" budget allocation.

3. Multi-Dimensional Profitability

The Pain: "Which regions are carrying the company and which are dragging us down?"

The Fix: Interactive Country and Year slicers. Transform a flat Statement of Changes in Equity into a dynamic tool that identifies top-performing territories in two clicks.

## Summary
Automated Reporting: Eliminated manual data entry by architecting a Star Schema from raw General Ledger data.

Executive Metrics: Surfaced critical KPIs including EBITDA, PBIT, and Sales FTP for instant health checks.

Trend Analysis: Visualized a 3-year performance window (2018-2020) to identify long-term growth patterns and seasonal anomalies.
